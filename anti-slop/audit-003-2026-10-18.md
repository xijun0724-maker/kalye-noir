## Findings

### 17. LOW (R-19 / R-31) The marquee loop has no written reason

`global.css:84` runs `.marquee` on `kn-marquee 30s linear infinite`, and `index.astro:94` renders the press-run banner with it. Everything about the handling is right: it pauses on `hover` and `focus-within`, it is `motion-reduce:animate-none`, and the global `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block disables it along with the `.reveal` rise. What is missing is the reason. `DESIGN.md` names the marquee as where registration red appears, but never says why the band moves, so a reviewer cannot tell a deliberate press-run device from a decorative loop. One line in `DESIGN.md` closes it.

### 18. MEDIUM (R-08 / R-06) The checkout terms block is untranslated courier copy, arrow and all

`checkout.astro:198-210` renders `orderTerms` and `deliveryLines` from `src/data/shipping.ts:63-74`. Three things break the UI rules, all in one block:

1. `deliveryIntro` opens with a `→` inside a sentence: "→ NOTE that delivery to your address (from date of pick-up) would be:". That is R-08 with the arrow in body prose rather than on a control, which also contradicts the arrow rule now written into `DESIGN.md`.
2. `deliveryLines` prefixes every row with a literal `*` while each row is already an `<li>` inside a `<ul>`: "`* 3 Business days for METRO MANILA address`". A doubled bullet used as a manual marker.
3. The copy shouts caps inside sentences and reads as a lifted courier notice, not the site voice: "2 - 3 BUSINESS DAYS, weekends and holidays NOT included", "NOTE: Please check your item upon receipt", "* Please prepare exact amount for your payment."

These strings look like the courier's own notice, which makes this an owner wording question rather than a silent rewrite (same protocol as the em dash decision in audit-002). Two paths: keep the facts, rewrite the four strings into the site's voice with the arrow and asterisks gone, or keep the wording verbatim and fix only the arrow and the doubled bullets.
