## Findings

### 31. MEDIUM: the thank-you page promises more than the email sends

`src/pages/order-thank-you.astro` tells the customer: "A print confirmation and exact courier dispatch timing will reach your inbox shortly." The email that now arrives right away confirms the order and shows the estimated delivery window for the province (for example "5 – 7 business days"). It does not give an exact dispatch time. Customers will read "exact" as a promise the first email doesn't keep (R-36: no claims the product does not back up).

Fix options: change the line to something like "A copy of your order is on its way to your inbox." Or keep the line and send a second, manual email with the dispatch time once the parcel is booked.

## Checked, no finding

- **Customer email copy:** plain greeting, a list of what was received, one next step that depends on the payment method, and a reply route. No buzzwords, invented facts, closing filler, or em dashes. The GCash line ("We will check your GCash / Maya payment, then send your parcel") matches the checkout terms. It does not add a new promise.
- **Section labels** (`ITEMS`, `DELIVER TO`, `NOTE`): these are uppercase labels in a plain-text receipt, not uppercase used for emphasis inside a sentence. Allowed.
- **Code comment** ("Only the checkout form carries a cart; incident reports also trigger this event."): it explains why the early return exists, which the code alone doesn't show (R-31). Keep it.
