# Surface brief — Shop (`/products`)

Mode: **Persuade** (storefront catalogue; the working form of the category's action is add-to-cart).
Scope: the product catalogue and order path; shares the home world.

Audience / job: a buyer who has decided the label is worth it, now picking pieces and sizes.
Action: add shirts / the pouch to the cart, then send the order request.
Content: `tee-medusa.png`, `tee-maynila.png`, `tee-kalyenoir.png`, `pouch.png`. Pricing confirmed per order.
Constraints: keep the existing localStorage cart, the drawer, the size selectors and the Netlify order form. Mobile-first.

Chosen direction: the same poster wall, read as a printed catalogue — each garment a mounted plate with its poster caption.

## Direction contract

**THESIS** — The catalogue is a printed poster run: every garment is a mounted plate with a coordinate-and-caption block, not a floating card on a neutral grid. It refuses the soft-shadowed rounded product card.

**OWN-WORLD** — Cream ground, ink plates, red hairline rules, mono captions, Anton names, square edges — the home world, read as a catalogue spread.

**STORY** — The visitor compares the three designs and the pouch quickly, picks sizes, adds to cart, and sends the order to be confirmed.

**FIRST VIEWPORT** — A catalogue masthead (title, count, coordinate rule) over the first two garment plates; the cart button stays in the masthead; the order form waits at the foot.

**FORM** — Poster wall as catalogue. Seed key: pinned.

**FINISH** — unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
