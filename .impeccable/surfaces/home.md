# Surface brief — Home (`/`)

Mode: **Persuade**. Scope: the storefront landing; whole-world redesign (replaces the incumbent Odyssey-poster look).

Audience / job: a Manila buyer arriving from a Facebook or Instagram promo, deciding in seconds whether this label is worth an order.
Action: land on **/products** (SHOP NOW) or open the order path (ORDER YOURS).
Proof / content: the brand's real poster art and garment mockups in `public/assets/images/brand/`. No fabricated prices, stock or testimonials.
Constraints: keep nav, cart and the order path working; mobile-first; the provided artwork is the hero material.

Chosen direction: the Manila wheatpaste poster wall — the label's own poster series pasted and layered, with the shop action set into it.
Seed key: **pinned** (the brief pins the world to the supplied poster art; concept-seed was unavailable in this environment).

## Direction contract

**THESIS** — The storefront is the brand's poster wall: its real posters pasted up and layered, the buy action mounted into the wall. It refuses the category's split hero (copy left, product right, badge row) and the row of equal cards.

**OWN-WORLD** — Newsprint cream ground, ink-black plates, one registration-red hairline, a sparing manila-sun accent. Heavy condensed caps (Anton) for display, wide-tracked mono (Azeret Mono) for coordinate/barcode labels, Archivo for body. Square corners. Motifs: four-point sparkle, coordinate ticks, barcode, wireframe globe, vertical city tabs.

**STORY** — The visitor reads a real Manila streetwear identity with a point of view, believes the shirts and pouch are worth ordering, and taps SHOP NOW.

**FIRST VIEWPORT** — Cream field. A giant stacked KALYE NOIR wordmark bleeds off the left edge; the KALYENOIR poster plate sits tall on the right with taped corners; the MANILA NEVER SLEEPS plate overlaps low and counter-rotated; coordinate and barcode micro-labels mark the corners; SHOP NOW (primary plaque) and ORDER YOURS sit under the wordmark.

**FORM** — Poster-wall collage (Persuade). Seed key: pinned.

**FINISH** — unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
